    Privacy, Local Data & Legal Notice

    100% On-Device Processing

    Buskero runs completely inside your browser with zero backend servers, zero analytics, zero tracking cookies, and a strict connect-src 'none' Content Security Policy that blocks outbound network connections.

    Microphone & Web MIDI Data

    When enabled for Violin or Saxophone, microphone audio is processed transiently in RAM via Web Audio (AudioWorklet + Web Worker) solely for real-time YIN pitch detection. Audio waveforms are never recorded, saved to disk, or sent off your device. Hardware Web MIDI events are read locally for note matching.

    Local Browser Storage (Under Your Control)

    • Uploaded MIDI Songs (IndexedDB): Custom .mid / .midi files you import remain stored locally in your browser so they stay ready across reloads.
    • Preferences & Personal Scores (localStorage): Active instrument, folder tree state, 2D/3D view mode, mic & horn calibration offsets, track transpose settings, and your own personal-best track scores are stored locally only.
